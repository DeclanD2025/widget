# 07 — Roles & Player Profiles

A position is *where* a player lines up; a role is *what he is asked to do*. Two players in
the same position can have opposite jobs. This chapter is the catalogue of roles and their
archetypes — the pieces that execute the tactics in Chapters 03–06.

Each entry: **what the role does · key attributes · archetype.**

---

## Goalkeepers

- **Sweeper-keeper** — defends the space behind a high line as an extra outfielder, and
  starts attacks with his feet. *Attributes:* reading of danger, passing range, composure.
  *Archetype:* Manuel Neuer, Ederson.
- **Shot-stopper / line keeper** — stays closer to goal, prioritises shot-stopping and
  command of the box over distribution. *Attributes:* reflexes, positioning, aerial
  dominance.

---

## Defenders

### Centre-backs
- **Ball-playing CB** — initiates build-up, breaks lines with passes, steps into midfield.
  *Attributes:* passing, composure under press, decision-making.
- **Stopper / no-nonsense CB** — wins duels, defends the box, attacks crosses. *Attributes:*
  aerial ability, tackling, aggression, positioning.
- **Wide CB (in a back three)** — defends and also steps out wide or carries the ball
  forward into midfield. *Attributes:* mobility, 1v1 defending wide, passing.
- **Libero / sweeper (historic & revived)** — a spare defender behind the line who reads
  danger and brings the ball out.

### Full-backs & wing-backs
- **Full-back (orthodox)** — defends the flank, supports the winger, overlaps. *Attributes:*
  stamina, 1v1 defending, crossing.
- **Inverted full-back** — steps into central midfield in possession to build numbers and
  improve rest defence. *Attributes:* positional sense, passing, ball security.
- **Attacking full-back** — primarily an attacking outlet who provides width and crosses.
- **Wing-back** — the engine of a back-five system: full width of the pitch, defends as a
  fifth defender and attacks as a fifth forward. *Attributes:* exceptional stamina, end
  product, defensive discipline.

## Midfielders

The richest variety of roles; the midfield is where games are usually controlled.

- **Single pivot (No. 6 / anchor / regista).** Sits in front of the defence, screens, and
  organises distribution. A *destroyer* breaks up play; a *regista* (deep-lying playmaker)
  dictates tempo from deep. *Attributes:* positioning, passing, reading the game.
- **Double pivot.** Two holding midfielders sharing screening and building duties; one may
  be more defensive, the other more progressive. Adds security and build-up angles.
- **Box-to-box (No. 8).** Covers ground end to end — defends, progresses, and arrives in the
  box. *Attributes:* stamina, all-round quality. *Archetype:* the complete midfielder.
- **Mezzala (half-space No. 8).** An 8 who plays high in the half-space, combining and
  arriving late in the box. *Attributes:* off-ball movement, combination play, finishing.
- **Segundo volante.** A defensive midfielder who breaks forward to support attacks — common
  in back-three systems.
- **No. 10 / attacking midfielder / playmaker.** Operates between the lines, the chief
  creator. *Attributes:* vision, first touch in tight space, final pass. *Archetype:* the
  classic enganche / trequartista.

---

## Forwards

- **No. 9 / centre-forward (complete).** Leads the line, scores, and links play.
- **Target man.** A big, strong striker who holds the ball up, wins aerial duels, and
  brings others into play; the focal point of direct football.
- **Poacher / fox in the box.** Lives off chances inside the box; movement and finishing
  over link play. *Archetype:* the pure goalscorer.
- **False 9.** A "striker" who drops into midfield, vacating the centre-back's zone and
  dragging him out (or leaving him with no one to mark), creating chaos and space for
  runners. *Attributes:* passing, movement, intelligence. *Archetype:* Messi under
  Guardiola, Firmino under Klopp.
- **Pressing forward.** A striker whose first job is to lead the press and set traps from
  the front. *Attributes:* work rate, pressing angles, stamina.
- **Winger.** Holds width, beats his man 1v1, delivers crosses. *Attributes:* pace,
  dribbling, end product.
- **Inside forward.** A wide forward who cuts inside onto his stronger foot to shoot and
  combine centrally (e.g. a left-footer on the right). *Attributes:* finishing, dribbling
  inside.
- **Second striker.** Plays off the main forward, between the lines and the box.

---

## How roles interact with shape and style

- A 4-3-3 *with two mezzalas* attacks the half-spaces; *with two pivots* it controls and
  builds. Same formation, different football — driven by the **roles**.
- Player profiles are the bridge between the tactics manual and the team-sheet: when you
  scout a fixture, ask not "what formation?" but "what is each key player asked to *do*, and
  who can stop him?"

> **For prediction:** mismatches between roles decide games. An attacking full-back with a
> weak defensive profile against an elite inside forward is a goal waiting to happen — and
> it is invisible if you only look at the formation.
