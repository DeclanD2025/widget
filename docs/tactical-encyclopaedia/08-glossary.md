# 08 — Glossary

An A–Z of the key terms used throughout this encyclopaedia. Cross-references point to the
chapter where a concept is explained in full.

---

### A
- **Automatism** — a rehearsed, repeated pattern of movement that a team executes without
  thinking (e.g. a fixed rotation in build-up). Seeing the *same* pattern twice means it is
  a plan, not chance. *(Ch. 10)*
- **Attacking transition** — the phase immediately after winning the ball. *(Ch. 05)*

### B
- **Back-pass trigger** — a common cue to start pressing: the opponent plays backwards.
  *(Ch. 04)*
- **Block (high / mid / low)** — how high up the pitch a team defends. *(Ch. 04)*
- **Box midfield** — a 2+2 midfield structure (e.g. via inverted full-backs) hard to press.
  *(Ch. 01, 03)*

### C
- **Catenaccio** — the classic Italian sweeper-based defensive system. *(Ch. 02)*
- **Channel** — one of the five vertical lanes of the pitch. *(Ch. 00)*
- **Compactness** — short distances between players, horizontally and vertically. *(Ch. 04)*
- **Counterpress (gegenpress)** — winning the ball back immediately after losing it.
  *(Ch. 02, 05)*
- **Cover shadow** — using your body to block the passing lane behind you, defending two
  opponents at once. *(Ch. 04)*
- **Cut-back** — a low ball pulled back from the byline to the penalty-spot area; a
  very high-value chance. *(Ch. 03)*

### D
- **Defensive transition** — the phase immediately after losing the ball. *(Ch. 05)*
- **Direct play** — moving the ball forward quickly with longer passes. *(Ch. 02)*
- **Double pivot** — two holding midfielders side by side. *(Ch. 01, 07)*

### F
- **False 9** — a centre-forward who drops into midfield to create space and overloads.
  *(Ch. 07)*
- **Final third** — the attacking third of the pitch. *(Ch. 00)*

### G
- **Gegenpressing** — see counterpress. *(Ch. 02)*
- **Give-and-go (one-two / wall pass)** — pass and run past your marker to receive again.
  *(Ch. 03)*

### H
- **Half-space** — the channel between the wing and the centre; the most valuable attacking
  zone. *(Ch. 00)*
- **High line** — a defensive line pushed up the pitch to compress space. *(Ch. 04)*

### I
- **Inverted full-back** — a full-back who steps into central midfield in possession.
  *(Ch. 03, 07)*
- **Isolation** — leaving a quality attacker 1v1 in space (the end of an overload-to-isolate
  move). *(Ch. 03)*

### L
- **Libero / sweeper** — a spare defender behind the defensive line. *(Ch. 07)*
- **Line-breaking pass** — a pass that eliminates opponents by going through a line. *(Ch. 03)*
- **Low block** — defending deep in your own third. *(Ch. 02, 04)*

### M
- **Man-marking** — assigning each defender a specific opponent. *(Ch. 04)*
- **Mezzala** — a No. 8 who plays high in the half-space. *(Ch. 07)*
- **Mid-block** — defending around the halfway line. *(Ch. 04)*

### O
- **Overload** — having more players than opponents in a zone. *(Ch. 00)*
- **Overload to isolate** — overloading one side to free a 1v1 on the other. *(Ch. 03)*
- **Offside trap** — stepping the defensive line up in unison to catch attackers offside.
  *(Ch. 04)*

### P
- **Packing** — a metric counting how many opponents a pass or carry takes out of the game.
  *(Ch. 09)*
- **Positional play (Juego de Posición)** — controlling the game by occupying space to
  create superiorities. *(Ch. 02)*
- **PPDA** (passes allowed per defensive action) — a pressing-intensity metric; lower =
  more aggressive pressing. *(Ch. 09)*
- **Press trigger** — the cue that launches a coordinated press. *(Ch. 04)*
- **Pivot** — the deepest midfielder who screens the defence and distributes. *(Ch. 07)*

### R
- **Regista** — a deep-lying playmaker who dictates tempo from in front of the defence.
  *(Ch. 07)*
- **Relationism** — a style prioritising player relationships/combinations over fixed zones.
  *(Ch. 02)*
- **Rest attack** — attackers kept high while defending, ready for the next transition.
  *(Ch. 03, 05)*
- **Rest defence** — players kept behind the ball while attacking, to stop counters.
  *(Ch. 04, 05)*
- **Rondo** — a keep-ball training drill that teaches positional-play principles. *(Ch. 02)*

### S
- **Salida lavolpiana** — a holding midfielder dropping between the centre-backs in build-up.
  *(Ch. 01, 03)*
- **Second ball** — the loose ball after a duel, header or clearance; winning it is a
  tactical priority. *(Ch. 04, 06)*
- **Superiority (numerical / positional / qualitative)** — the three advantages positioning
  aims to create. *(Ch. 00)*
- **Switch of play** — a long diagonal pass to the opposite, less-crowded channel. *(Ch. 03)*

### T
- **Tactical foul** — a deliberate foul to stop a dangerous counter-attack. *(Ch. 05)*
- **Target man** — a strong forward who holds the ball and wins aerial duels. *(Ch. 07)*
- **Third-man combination** — A→B opens a pass to C, whom A could not reach directly. *(Ch. 03)*
- **Tiki-taka** — possession-heavy short passing; distinct from purposeful positional play.
  *(Ch. 02)*
- **Total Football** — the 1970s Dutch system of universal interchange and pressing. *(Ch. 02)*
- **Transition** — the moment of change between having and not having the ball. *(Ch. 05)*
- **Turnover map** — a mental/visual map of where each team wins and loses the ball. *(Ch. 05, 10)*

### W
- **Wing-back** — a wide player covering the whole flank in a back-five system. *(Ch. 07)*

### X
- **xG (expected goals)** — the probability a given chance is scored; the standard measure
  of chance quality. *(Ch. 09)*
- **xT (expected threat)** — a model valuing each action by how much it raises the chance of
  scoring soon after. *(Ch. 09)*

### Z
- **Zone 14** — the central area at the top of the box; the prime playmaking square. *(Ch. 00)*
- **Zonal marking** — defending space rather than a specific man. *(Ch. 04, 06)*
